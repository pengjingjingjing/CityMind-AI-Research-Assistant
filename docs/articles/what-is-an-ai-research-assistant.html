What Is an AI Research Assistant?

By Peng Jing · Published August 2026

An AI research assistant is a software tool that uses a language model to help organize questions, summarize supplied information, propose research structures and identify next tasks. It can accelerate early-stage work, but it does not automatically provide verified evidence or reliable citations.

What can an AI research assistant do?

  • Turn a broad topic into clearer research questions;
  • Create an outline or briefing structure;
  • Summarize text supplied by the user;
  • Suggest evidence categories and data gaps;
  • Prepare presentation headings and follow-up tasks.

What can it not guarantee?

It cannot guarantee factual accuracy, current policy information, complete evidence or valid citations unless the system is connected to verified sources and the results are still reviewed by a human.

How is CityMind different from a general chat prompt?

CityMind uses a fixed research-brief structure and explicit rules for missing evidence. The goal is not a long conversational answer but a reviewable starting document.

When should the output be verified?

Always verify policies, statistics, dates, cases, standards and citations before formal use. High-stakes decisions require qualified professional review.
