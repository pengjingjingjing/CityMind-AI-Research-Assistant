Prompt design and content quality

CityMind Prompt Design

By Peng Jing · Published August 2026

CityMind currently uses a structured single-stage prompt. The prompt specifies the assistant role, content order, writing style and rules for uncertain or unsupported information.

Design goal

The goal is to produce a stable research starting point rather than an unrestricted conversational response.

Core prompt rules

  • Do not invent exact statistics, policies, citations or case results;
  • Do not claim a source was checked unless the user supplied it;
  • Separate evidence, preliminary judgment and recommendation;
  • List missing evidence as data gaps;
  • End with concrete next research tasks.

Why use fixed headings?

Fixed headings make outputs easier to compare across topics, support testing and reduce the chance that important sections disappear inside a long answer.

Current limitation

A single model call can still produce repetition or uneven section depth. The planned next version will generate and validate an outline before expanding each section.

What should be tested?

  • Section completeness;
  • Repetition and topic drift;
  • Unsupported factual claims;
  • Usefulness of data-gap warnings;
  • Consistency across different research topics.
